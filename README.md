### 🧭 About Me

I'm a backend engineer focused on **distributed, event-driven systems** — the kind that move messages reliably at scale. At **The Legal Leads**, I build AWS backends that fan events out through SNS/SQS, orchestrate them with Step Functions, and process them across Lambda and ECS, all while guaranteeing correctness under at-least-once delivery with idempotent consumers and dead-letter queues.

Before that, at **Deloitte**, I helped migrate a monolithic financial-services platform (32K+ accounts) into containerized, service-oriented components on Kubernetes with Jenkins CI/CD.

- 🔭 Currently building **[Wanderful](https://github.com/mahijeetreddy/Wanderful)** — a fault-tolerant LLM orchestration layer across 5+ providers
- 🌱 Deepening my work in **AWS Certified Developer**, **Databricks AI Associate**, and **OCI AI Professional** territory
- 🎓 M.S. Computer Science, Cal State Fullerton — where I also taught distributed-systems architecture labs
- 📫 Reach me at **mahijeetreddygaddam@gmail.com**
